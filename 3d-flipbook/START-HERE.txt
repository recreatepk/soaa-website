MODERN 3D FLIPBOOK — START HERE

1) Copy the entire "modern-3d-flipbook" folder into your website project.
   You may rename the folder to "3d-flipbook".

2) Put your company-profile PDF into:
   3d-flipbook/pdf/

3) Delete PLACE-PDF-HERE.txt if you want.

4) Open in browser through your web server:
   http://localhost/your-project/3d-flipbook/

5) Add this to the website header:
   <a href="/3d-flipbook/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="btn btn-primary">Company Profile</a>

6) Optional: edit config.php to change viewer brand name, title, accent color,
   back URL, download button, sound default, or a fixed PDF filename.

IMPORTANT:
Use Apache/Nginx/PHP/XAMPP. Do not double-click index.php as a file:// page.
